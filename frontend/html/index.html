One conversation.
Four deliberate stages.

Each stage adds a different kind of context, keeping the interview focused from preparation through reflection.

03 / Operating principles

Useful intelligence
needs good boundaries.

The architecture is only part of the story. The experience also depends on how information is handled, presented, and reviewed.

01

Keep the human in the decision

Present analysis as decision support. The hiring team remains responsible for interpretation and outcomes.

◎
02

Make context traceable

Separate interview evidence from external research so reviewers can understand where information came from.

⌁
03

Design for consistent review

Use shared evaluation criteria to make feedback easier to compare, explain, and revisit.

≡

Good tooling should make the reasoning easier to inspect, not harder to see.
